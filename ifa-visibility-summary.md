# UK IFA AI Visibility Research Study

**Date:** 9 March 2026
**Model:** GPT-4o
**Firms Tested:** 149
**UK Regions:** 10
**API Calls Made:** 30 (10 regions x 3 prompt variants)

---

## Key Finding

**79% of UK IFA firms are invisible to AI.**

When GPT-4o was asked to recommend independent financial advisers across 10 UK regions using 3 different prompt styles (30 total queries), only **32** out of **149** real IFA firms were ever mentioned.

- Firms mentioned by AI: **32** (21%)
- Firms completely invisible: **117** (79%)

## Methodology

This study tested whether GPT-4o would organically recommend real UK IFA firms when asked for financial adviser recommendations. Three prompt variants were used per region:

1. "Can you recommend a good independent financial adviser in [region]?"
2. "Who are the best independent financial advisers in [region]?"
3. "I'm looking for a trusted financial adviser in [region]. Can you suggest some reputable IFA firms?"

A firm was counted as "found" if its name (or a known variant/alias) appeared in any of the 30 responses. Word-boundary matching was used to prevent false positives from generic terms.
